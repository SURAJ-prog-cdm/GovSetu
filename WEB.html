Why GovSetu?

Traditional e-District portals like Meeseva require you to visit a center, fill physical forms, and wait for processing. GovSetu solves the interoperability problem by:

  • Keeping one canonical citizen record (Fill Once)
  • Using AI to map your data to any department's schema (Use Everywhere)
  • Providing a tamper-evident consent ledger for compliance (DPDP Act)
